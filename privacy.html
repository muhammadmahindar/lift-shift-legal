Lift Shift privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

In short

  • We collect no data. No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing about you or your play is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Saves and settings are stored only inside the app.
  • Reminders are local. If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled on your device. No server and no push service are involved.
  • No ads and no purchases.

Who we are

Lift Shift is a single-player elevator game made by muhammadmahindar. This policy covers the current version of the app on Android, iPhone and the web.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps the following in its own private storage on your device. None of it leaves your device.

DataWhy
Game progress: shifts, stars, Rush and Daily Rush scores, coins, upgrades, tower and rooms, missions and streak, unlocked liveries and buildingsSo you can carry on where you left off
Settings: volume, reduced motion, high contrast, extra time, game speed, notification choiceSo your preferences stick
A previous copy of your saveSo a damaged save can be recovered

Deleting the app deletes all of this. Your device's own backup may include the app's data (on Android, Google's device backup; on iPhone, iCloud or a computer backup) under Google's or Apple's privacy terms. Backups you export from Settings are files you choose where to keep; we never receive them.

Notifications

After your first Rush (never at first launch) the app may ask to show notifications. If you allow it, it schedules reminders on the device, such as "Your Daily Rush is ready" or a streak reminder. There is no remote server and no push token. You can turn them off at any time in your device's notification settings for Lift Shift.

Network use

All game files are bundled with the app, and the app makes no network requests of its own. The Daily Rush is the same for everyone because it is built from the date on your device, not downloaded.

In Credits & privacy, "Privacy policy" opens this page in your browser and "Contact support" opens your email app; those apps work under their own privacy terms. Sharing your Daily result uses your device's share sheet and sends only what you choose to share.

The web version downloads its files from the site hosting it and checks there for updates. That host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Third parties

No third-party advertising, analytics, social or tracking SDKs. The app uses open-source libraries that run entirely on the device: Capacitor (app shell, haptics, sharing, local notifications) and Three.js (3D graphics).

Future changes

If a future version ever offers anonymous gameplay statistics or crash reports, then before that version is released:

  • this policy will be updated, naming the provider and the exact data;
  • the Google Play Data safety section and the App Store privacy label will be updated to match;
  • sharing will be off until you turn it on in Settings, and you can turn it off again at any time;
  • the data will never be used for advertising or tracking, never sold, and never combined with data from other apps.

Your rights and contact

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Everything lives on your device and is removed when you delete the app.
