Quick help

  • How to play: tap a floor where people wait and the best lift goes. Tap it again to hurry it. In the game, Settings → How to play has the rest.
  • Keep your progress safe: Settings → Advanced → Export backup saves a file you can import on a new phone.
  • Reminders: they are optional and local. Turn them off in your phone's notification settings for Lift Shift.
  • Progress looks wrong? Settings → Advanced → Restore previous backup brings back the save before your latest one.

Privacy

No account, ads only when you tap to watch one, and anonymous play statistics you can switch off in Settings. Your saves stay on your device. Read the privacy policy.
